# Lawson Media Pitch Website

## Product intent
Replace the former PRESSRA demo with a client-facing pitch website for Lawson Media / Lawson Multimedia. The page must explain the confirmed public-web problems, a staged recovery and rebuild solution, the unified newsroom model, implementation options, timeline, total project cost, separate developer/build cost, and recurring support packages in a warm business tone.

## Architecture
Use the existing React + Vite application as a single responsive proposal page with semantic sections and anchor navigation. Keep all copy and pricing in `src/main.tsx`, with reusable components for evidence cards, architecture flow, phases, pricing cards, FAQ and CTA. Use `src/styles.css` for a distinctive editorial-consulting visual system. Keep `/manus-routes.json` synchronized with the single `/` route.

## Design direction
- Movement: editorial newsroom briefing with Ghanaian broadcast energy.
- Principles: evidence-led, calm under pressure, commercially clear, easy to scan.
- Palette: warm paper, deep ink, broadcast red, signal green, and amber warning accents.
- Layout: long-form vertical pitch with an asymmetric hero, evidence strip, connected-system diagram, and strong pricing band.
- Typography: DM Sans for operational copy and a high-contrast serif display face for the pitch headlines.
- Brand voice: friendly, direct and respectful; describe the current problem without insulting the existing team.

## Required content
1. Hero: one newsroom, every Lawson channel connected.
2. Confirmed situation: expired/parked Lawson Media Online domain, broken HTTPS/404 paths, Lawson Multimedia redirect, active Lawson TV/96.3 FM social output.
3. Business impact and immediate recovery actions.
4. Proposed unified newsroom and one-content/multi-channel workflow.
5. Public website features, newsroom dashboard, video/live/radio and distribution.
6. Technology options: custom React/Next or Astro public site with Payload/Directus headless CMS; WordPress-free path.
7. Three implementation phases and timeline.
8. Transparent pricing: audit/recovery, website rebuild, newsroom platform, full ecosystem, and monthly support separated from developer/build cost.
9. Friendly pitch close and next step.
